Delete your data

Elemental Fluffs: Fusion Jar · Sukoon Labs

Elemental Fluffs has no sign-up. It creates a random player ID so your progress can be backed up and your scores shown on leaderboards. You can delete that player ID and everything tied to it.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Elemental Fluffs and tap Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Erase my data.
  3. Tap Erase everything to confirm. You need an internet connection.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email sukoonlabsdev@gmail.com with the subject “Elemental Fluffs data deletion” and your username. We delete the data within 30 days and reply when it is done. We may ask for a detail only the owner would know, such as your best score, so we don’t delete someone else’s data.

What is deleted

  • Your player ID and its sign-in
  • Your backed-up progress: coins, collection, power-ups, cosmetics, missions and settings
  • Your username, and your entries on leaderboards, the Daily Challenge board and Weekly Leagues
  • Name reports you made
  • Your game on this phone (in-game deletion only)

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

What is kept

  • Purchases stay with your Google Play or App Store account so you can restore Remove Ads and other one-time items. Stores keep their own purchase records.
  • Analytics and crash reports are not linked to you once your player ID is deleted, and expire on their own (analytics within 14 months, crash reports in about 90 days).
  • Advertising data is held by Google; manage it at My Ad Center.
