Privacy Policy

Elemental Fluffs: Fusion Jar · Sukoon Labs · Effective 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Elemental Fluffs: Fusion Jar (“the game”), published by Sukoon Labs (“we”, “us”), collects, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as the game needs to work.

The short version

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game gives you a random player ID so your progress can be backed up and your scores can appear on leaderboards.
  • You pick a username. It is public on leaderboards and in Weekly Leagues, so please don’t use your real name.
  • We use Google Firebase to store your backup and scores, count how the game is played, and catch crashes.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law asks for it, you choose whether ads are personalised.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • You can erase everything we keep about you from inside the game: Settings → Erase my data.

Our analytics are not used for advertising: the game tells Google Analytics not to collect the advertising ID and not to use this data for ads. Firebase services process data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usernames and other players

Your username, avatar, frame, scores and (if you choose) country are shown to other players on leaderboards, the Daily Challenge board and in your Weekly League group. Usernames are checked against a filter and must be unique. Do not use your real name or anything that identifies you. If you report a name, we store which name you reported and your player ID so we can review it. We may rename or remove a username that breaks our Terms of Use.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads: a small banner at the top of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between the end of a run and its results, and optional videos you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are never shown in the middle of a run. They are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before personalised ads are shown, the game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form. You can change it anytime in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • US state privacy rights: where required, the same menu lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for ads.
  • iPhone tracking: on iPhone, the game asks permission before using the advertising identifier. Saying no does not change how the game plays.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone’s settings. Ads then become less personalised.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off the banner and full-screen ads. Optional reward videos stay available.
  • Reminders: optional. The game asks for notification permission only after your first run, and reminders are scheduled on your phone (at most one a day); nothing is sent to us. Turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your phone’s settings.
  • Sharing: share pictures are made on your phone and passed to the app you choose; that app’s own privacy policy then applies. The game does not read your photos or clipboard.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple’s App Store under their own privacy policies. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it. Purchases stay with your store account and can be restored after a reinstall.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Erase in the game: Settings → Erase my data deletes your backup, username, leaderboard and league entries and your player ID from our servers, then resets the game on your phone. Purchases are not affected.
  • Without the game: see Delete your data, or email us.
  • Your backup and scores are kept until you erase them. Old weekly and daily boards may be removed after they end.
  • Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months, and crash reports for about 90 days.
  • Uninstalling the game removes what it stored on your phone, but not your server backup, so it can come back if you reinstall on the same device. Use Erase my data first if you want it gone.

Where your data is processed

Firebase and AdMob are run by Google, which may process data on servers outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States, with safeguards required by law. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do in the game. For anything else, email us from the address below and include your username; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising data is handled by Google as described above.

Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the change.
