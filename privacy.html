Privacy Policy

One Line Draw · Sukoon Labs · Effective 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game One Line Draw (“the app”), published by Sukoon Labs (“we”, “us”), collects, why, and the choices you have. We built the app to need as little of your data as possible.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We don’t run servers that receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases (such as Remove Ads) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • If the app crashes or runs slowly, an anonymous technical report is sent so we can fix it.

Information stored on your device

Level progress, stars, skip tokens, sound and other settings, and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved locally on your phone so the game remembers where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress (purchases stay with your Google account and can be restored).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads: an optional video when you choose to revive, skip a level or get more undos, a small banner from level 6, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersShowing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud
IP address, used to estimate your general (approximate) locationShowing relevant ads, complying with local laws, preventing fraud
Interactions with ads (views, taps)Measuring ads and rewarding you when you finish a rewarded video
Device and app information, crash and diagnostic dataKeeping ads working and secure

This data is collected and processed by Google, encrypted in transit, and governed by Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Crash and performance reports

To find and fix bugs, the app uses EAS Observe by Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), a service that processes this data on our behalf. When the app crashes, shows an error, or loads a screen, it may send:

  • crash and error details (what went wrong in the app's code), and screen load times;
  • the app version, device model and operating system version;
  • a random ID created for this installation of the app — it is not linked to your name, account or advertising ID, and it resets if you reinstall the app.

These reports are used only to keep the app stable and fast — never for advertising — and are sent over encrypted connections. See Expo's privacy policy.

Anonymous play statistics

To make levels fair, the app records which level you finished, skipped or ran out of energy on, with the stars, number of attempts, mistakes, undos and time taken. These statistics use the same random installation ID as crash reports and are processed by EAS Observe. You can turn them off anytime in Settings → Share anonymous play stats.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before personalised ads are shown, the app asks for your consent using Google’s consent form. You can change or withdraw it anytime in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • US state privacy rights: where required, the same menu lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone’s settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads will then be less personalised.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off banner and full-screen ads. Optional rewarded videos stay available if you want their rewards.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google Payments’ privacy notice. The app only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on our own servers. Crash and performance reports are anonymous and kept by Expo only as long as needed to fix problems. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. For data collected by Google for advertising, use the choices above or Google’s tools at My Ad Center.

Security

The app communicates with Google’s services over encrypted connections. We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example analytics), we will update this page and the date above before releasing them.
